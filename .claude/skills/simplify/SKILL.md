# Simplify

Recent work accumulates cleverness. This skill reduces over-complexity and executes the cleanup with discipline, so the material is clearer for the GM at the table.

## When to run

- After a first version of the spec has been written.
- After finishing the execution of the spec.

## Principles

- **Preserve exact behavior.** The session must play out the same; only the presentation gets simpler.
- **Follow the conventions of the project and of the existing sessions.**
- **Clarity over cleverness.**
- **Show what was modified.**

## Process

1. **Understand before modifying** (Chesterton's fence): know why a card, branch, or rule exists before touching it. If you can't explain its purpose, ask or leave it.
2. **Identify signals**: deep nesting (3+ levels of conditions or sub-branches), a card doing two jobs, the same fact duplicated across cards, branches no player choice can reach, text the GM cannot scan mid-session.
3. **Apply incrementally**: one simplification at a time — never batch unrelated cleanups into a single change.
4. **Verify the result** after each step: Is it actually easier to read? Does it help at the table during the session? Is it coherent with the previous and the next sessions?

Finish with a short list of what was simplified and why.
